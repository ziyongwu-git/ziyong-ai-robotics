## Idea

A two-link arm lives on a flat plane. Forward kinematics turns joint angles into a hand position. Inverse kinematics goes the other way: you name a point, and you solve for the angles.

The hand can reach a ring of points. Inside the inner ring it cannot fold tightly enough. Outside the outer ring it cannot stretch far enough.
